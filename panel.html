Help & privacy

If no reply appears

The panel always shows a specific coded reason instead of asking you to paste a reply.

  • No adapter: Connect attaches the page adapter automatically. If Chrome blocks it, check this extension's Details → Site access for https://app.all-hands.dev and reconnect.
  • Control not found: the launcher and conversation pages are supported; settings, automations, MCP, skills and shared conversations have no chat to drive. If the site's markup moved, the capability line in Settings names the missing control.
  • Verification: if OpenHands shows a security check, complete it in the tab. Tracking pauses and resumes on its own once it passes — nothing is resent and nothing is bypassed.
  • Rate limit: follow the wait time OpenHands shows; capture stops rather than retrying.
  • Confirmation: an action confirmation blocks Send until you answer it, here or in the tab. High-risk actions are never confirmed for you.
  • Paused agent: tracking is held, not stopped. Resume the agent here or in the tab and capture continues.
  • Timed out or ambiguous: inspect OpenHands before resending — the message may already have been accepted.

Staged files are placed into the OpenHands composer immediately before your one Send click. Tool approvals, workspace settings and anything the adapter does not recognize stay in the OpenHands tab. Live text and step labels are previews, not the finished answer.

Chat stays in memory · no API key · no credential access · no network calls

Only theme, text size and accent are saved. Closing the side panel or disconnecting clears its transcript; your OpenHands conversation is untouched. OpenHands may continue a task already sent.

If you see a Copy prompt button or a reply-paste box, you have opened an older extension — not this automatic-only edition.
